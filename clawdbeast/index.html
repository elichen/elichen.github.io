    Build

      Kinematics use Jansen's published link ratios. Design rules come from SendCutSend's guidelines: DXF with closed contours, one part per file at 1:1 scale; holes at least the material thickness; 2× thickness from hole to edge; minimum part size per material. Nothing here has been cut and assembled yet, so treat the first build as a prototype.

Air Hockey RL Agent

Payoff-matrix PSRO + prioritized self-play policy

Training Methodology

The agent was initialized from the deterministic incumbent, then trained with PPO best responses against a payoff-matrix league. Prioritized fictitious self-play emphasizes both the approximate Nash mixture and difficult opponents, while dedicated exploiters expose weaknesses for later rounds.

Observation Space (12 features)

  • Own paddle: position (x, y), velocity (dx, dy)
  • Puck: position (x, y), velocity (dx, dy)
  • Opponent paddle: position (x, y), velocity (dx, dy)

Results

  • 136–73 with 91 draws against the incumbent over 300 side-balanced games
  • 60.5% match score, with a 95% interval of 55.9–65.1%
  • 72.0% against random and 87.5% against the scripted baseline
  • Human and AI paddles now use the same speed cap and movement smoothing

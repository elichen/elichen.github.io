# Miffy reference QA

Reviewed 9 September 2026. Reference images were inspected visually, not just inferred from search descriptions. They are reference-only and are not shipped as app assets.

## Visual references

- [Medicom Toy official store — UDF Dick Bruna (series 5), Walk Miffy](https://store.medicomtoy.co.jp/en/products/4530956157061). Primary 3D reference: broad cheeks, flattened chin, full upright ears, widely spaced oval eyes, shallow cross mouth, short orange dress, visible white feet.
- [Studio Brillantine — Miffy Figure orange](https://www.studiobrillantine.com/miffy-figure-orange/). Inspected the standing figure photograph to cross-check the neutral pose, short sleeves, white button, and head-to-body proportions. This is a retailer photograph, not an official character construction sheet.
- [Miffy official site — Miffy Bunny](https://www.miffy.com/miffy-bunny). Character context.

The result remains a stylized fan-made 3D interpretation, not an exact reproduction of an official model.

## Corrections made

| First version | Revised model |
| --- | --- |
| Spherical head and rounded chin | Lathed profile with broader cheeks and a flatter chin |
| Narrow, tapering ears | Fuller ears, slightly splayed, with rounded tips |
| Close-set eyes | Wider spacing and slightly taller oval dots |
| Almost square X mouth | Wider, shallower cross following the face surface |
| Long, pale orange cone dress | Shorter, stronger orange dress with a tiny white button |
| Feet mostly hidden by the dress | Clearly visible rounded white feet |

## Downloadable models evaluated

- [ployc18 — miffy](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/miffy-24743263792646df99be0a7f5d3a03db): listing offers a download under CC Attribution; 178.5k triangles. Not imported: considerably more geometry than needed for this small character; animation rig not verified.
- [Luna — Miffy Head](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/miffy-head-ba26a98dfaab458d95037e7c11f494a6): listing offers a download under CC Attribution; 4.2k triangles. A lightweight option, but head-only, so it would not replace the animated character.
- [CGTrader — Miffy Rabbit](https://www.cgtrader.com/3d-models/animal/mammal/miffy-rabbit): listing includes Blender/GLB/FBX/OBJ/STL and 69,668 triangles; explicitly not rigged or animation-ready. Not imported.

These are third-party models, not verified official Mercis production assets. No third-party model was downloaded, purchased, or incorporated. The app retains its locally generated, separately articulated geometry.

## Validation

- Compare the idle pose to the standing reference: silhouette, face spacing, mouth angle, dress length, and feet.
- Inspect the pulling pose: hands still surround the carrot after shortening the body.
- Check the complete harvest returns to idle and enables the button.
- Check browser console and JavaScript syntax.

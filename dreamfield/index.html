Is it really learning style?

Yes, within a deliberately small definition. Each source stroke is resampled and smoothed into an estimated intended path. The network learns the remaining local deviations plus width and color. At generation time it receives only a different subject scaffold and fresh noise—not your source coordinates or pixels.

# Visual references and data

## Tuxedo Sam

Online image search and direct visual inspection of the following official Sanrio illustrations informed the procedural model. Reference images are not bundled or displayed in the app.

- [Sanrio Japan character page](https://www.sanrio.co.jp/characters/tuxedosam/)
- [Official Japanese character illustration](https://www.sanrio.co.jp/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/list-tuxedosam.png)
- [Official Sanrio China character illustration](https://www.sanrio.com.cn/m/images/family/19/d1.png)
- [Sanrio US profile](https://www.sanrio.com/collections/tuxedosam)

The critical traits checked against the images are the broad round blue silhouette; eyes on blue rather than in the white belly; white belly beginning beneath the beak; small black oval eyes; compact yellow beak; flat yellow feet; pink bow tie; small tilted white sailor cap with pink band and ribbon tails. The satchel and travel setting are original additions. The 3D interpretation uses soft sculptural shading rather than the reference artwork's black outlines.

Tuxedosam is a Sanrio character. This is an unofficial fan-made experiment, not an official Sanrio product.

## Geography

[Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) public-domain 1:110m land polygons, downloaded from [natural-earth-vector](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson).

Landmark positions use approximate city coordinates. Tuxedo Island is fictional; its Antarctic placement is illustrative. Trees, mountains, and landmarks are stylized miniatures, not geographic measurements. The route is a storybook walk that also crosses oceans.

## Runtime

[Babylon.js](https://www.babylonjs.com/), version 9.25.0, Apache 2.0. All meshes, textures, animation, and audio are produced in code. No Three.js is used.
